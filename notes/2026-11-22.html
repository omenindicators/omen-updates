Big update
One look across every Omen tool.
The November update brings every Omen tool onto one shared look. The old drop-down menus are now cards with picture tiles, every dashboard has the same size card, and you can drag a dashboard to the other side of the chart. Omen Rack now opens empty and adds tools for you. Omen Trend lets you change its 12 timeframes right on the dashboard, and Omen Draw keeps your colors in your templates. Two names changed as well: Position Sizer is now Omen Sync, and Omen MTF CE is now Omen Trend. They're the same tools under the same license, and your settings carry over.

IN EVERY OMEN TOOL
 
Cards, size and placementNEW
In Omen Sync, Trend, Draw, Layers, Ink and Core.
▸Cards in place of menus. Every choice opens a card with picture tiles. The one in use is lit, and hovering a tile says what it does.
▸One help card (?) with Ask Oi, Contact Mike, Report a problem and Quick Start.
▸One size card (Aa). DASHBOARD SIZE zooms the whole dashboard, DASHBOARD TEXT makes only the words bigger (long lines wrap instead of covering anything), and CHART TEXT sizes the words on your chart. The sliders click from step to step, and one key puts everything back to the defaults.
▸Move a dashboard to the other side. Drag it by its grip to the other half of the chart and it moves into that side's column, right where you let go.
▸Control Center: every Omen tool now sits under one Omen Indicators line in the Tools and New menus.
WHAT'S NEW
 
Omen RackNEW 1.0.0.4 → 1.0.0.5
Build your Rack from the Rack.
▸+ TOOL adds or removes Omen tools on the picked chart, with no trip to NinjaTrader's Indicators window. MORE OMEN TOOLS shows the ones you don't have yet.
▸Start empty: open it from Control Center (New › Omen Indicators › Omen Rack) before any tool is on a chart. Every open chart shows as a chip, dimmed until it has a tool.
▸Every tab counts: each tab of every chart window gets its own chip.
▸Click a chip and that chart comes to the front, with the Rack on top of it.
▸Removing the last tool no longer closes the Rack. Only its X does.
▸Fixed: the Rack could open off your screen after NinjaTrader closed while it was minimized.
Omen Trend (was Omen MTF CE)NEW 1.0.0.8 → 1.0.0.9
Your timeframes, set on the dashboard.
▸Change the 12 timeframes right on the dashboard: EDIT on TIMEFRAMES IN THE SCORE, pick a timeframe (or type your own, like 7h), then APPLY.
▸The flip alert's WATCH and CONFIRM are picked on the dashboard from the timeframes Omen Trend loads, so they always match. If one ever doesn't, the dashboard offers a one-click fix.
▸CHART TEXT sizes the alert tags, the score and the strip labels together.
▸New charts start with WickWatch confirms off, so every timeframe's vote counts.
Omen DrawIMPROVED 1.0.1.5 → 1.0.1.6
Your colors, kept.
▸Line colors on the dashboard: SETUP › LINES › COLORS for Daily, 4H, Prev day, Session, Swept and The Draw. Each chart keeps its own, for dark and light charts.
▸Templates keep your colors. Re-save your templates once (right-click › Update) so they pick them up.
▸4-hour and daily levels look back 9 months by default, so the next 4-hour low is still there after a hard week.
▸Fixed: long DRAW and pool labels no longer wrap onto a second line.
Omen Sync (was Omen Position Sizer)IMPROVED 1.0.2.2 → 1.0.2.3
Your chart, uncovered.
▸Price tags in the price axis: the tags on your entry, stop, target and order lines sit in the axis, like NinjaTrader's own, so they no longer cover the latest bars.
▸A line out of view shows as a faded flag on the chart's edge, with ▲ or ▼ and its price. Drag it and the line comes to your mouse.
▸FLATTEN EVERYTHING and the GUARD plan picker open cards. CHART TEXT sizes the flag text, now 12 pt on new charts.
Omen LayersIMPROVED 1.0.0.7 → 1.0.0.8
▸QUADRANTS: Off, Quadrants or Octants as pictures, on a zone's color card and on the TUNE knob.
▸New WINDOW knob on the TARGETS RANGE row picks the dealing range's window: Asia, London, NY kill zone, NY AM, PM, RTH or your own.
▸PD ARRAYS + opens a card of timeframes. CHART TEXT sizes the labels on the chart.
Omen InkIMPROVED 1.0.1.2 → 1.0.1.3
▸CHART TEXT sets one size for every Ink drawing's words on the chart, from 80% to 200%. 100% is each drawing's own size.
▸The Ink bar's ··· opens a card: where the bar sits, how its buttons line up, and what it shows.
▸Right-click a pinned button for Unpin, Move left and Move right.
Omen CoreIMPROVED 1.0.0.1 → 1.0.0.2
▸A new move card: Pane, Chart column, Core window, Omen Rack or Own window, as pictures.
▸MOVE ALL in the Omen Core window moves every Omen Core indicator on the chart in one click.
▸Fixed: RSI, MACD or ADX on the price panel no longer stretch the price scale.
HOW TO UPDATE
 
1Open your Gumroad library and download the new zip for each tool you own.
2In NinjaTrader: Tools > Import > NinjaScript Add-On and pick the zip.
3While you're flat: reload every chart that has an Omen tool (right-click the chart > Reload NinjaScript, or press F5 on it), or restart NinjaTrader.
Open my Gumroad library →
Your update window in NinjaTrader offers one-click install. Omen Accounts and Omen Switch get the new cards too, and Omen Switch fixes its labels tag on charts with several tabs.
