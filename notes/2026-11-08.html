Big update
The Omen tools, redesigned
This is our biggest round yet: a full UX redesign across the Omen tools, so every dash looks and works the same way. Position Sizer, Draw, MTF CE and Layers get the new screen keys and a settings rail, and templates arrive in Position Sizer, Ink, Draw, MTF CE and Layers, so you save a whole setup once and switch to it in one click. Omen Ink gets templates and a brand-new quick bar, Omen Switch gets a new settings window, and there's a new Customize window everywhere.

IN EVERY OMEN TOOL
 
Customize & displayNEW
In Omen Position Sizer, Ink, Draw, MTF CE, Layers and Accounts.
▸A new Customize window. A live preview sits on top and lights up the part you point at. One rail takes you to Skin, Colors, Modules, Knobs, Buttons, Picture, Glow, Size and Sets: one page, one job.
▸Skins work the way you'd expect. Picking a skin gives you that skin whole, and UNDO brings your own edits back. Copy a look to your other dashes on purpose, from SAVE.
▸New dashes start with the last look you used, background picture included. Dash size is kept per dash and per chart.
▸Sharper words at any size. Lit keys, resized dashes and the Omen Rack window keep crisp text, and card titles are bigger and easier to read.
▸Report a problem, right from the tool. Pick Report a problem from the ? menu (in Omen Switch and Omen Dash too), or press Report it on the red strip if a tool ever hits an error. You see exactly what will be sent before anything goes, and nothing is sent until you press Send.
▸More Omen tools in the update window. When updates arrive, the window also shows the Omen tools you don't own yet, each with a 7-day free trial. "Not for me" hides one for good.
TOOL BY TOOL
 
Omen Position SizerREDESIGN
Built for every trade, now cleaner and faster.
▸TRADE / SETUP and CONTRACTS are screen keys in your skin's display colors. Lit means on, every time.
▸Quick keys on the display for paired orders, LINK STOPS and SNAP, each with an icon and a word.
▸SETUP rail: all 8 settings pages down the side, with each value right on its key.
▸Templates: save your risk, stop, contracts, entry, targets, trail and copy settings, then switch from the display in one click. UNDO and Ctrl+Z, and a star for your default.
▸Only orders get raised buttons now (BUY, SELL, ADD). REVERSE, BREAK-EVEN, CLOSE PART and CANCEL open a panel that shows exactly what will happen, with a two-click send.
▸REVERSE gets its own panel: the new side and size, and what it cancels and closes.
▸Sharper words on every key, including the FLAGS and ENTRY tiles.
Omen InkNEW
Templates and the new quick bar.
▸Templates for every drawing tool. Save a look ("Bull FVG", "Bear FVG", "Suspension block") and apply its color, width, fill, line, CE and text in one click.
▸TEMPLATES is the first page on the rail, with big picture tiles. Each tool keeps its own list, with groups and a default.
▸UNDO and Ctrl+Z after you apply, update, replace or delete a template, and EDITED shows when a drawing no longer matches its template.
▸The quick bar: select a drawing and a bar floats beside it, never over the drawing or its handles. Your template pictures are right there: hover to preview, click to apply.
▸RECENT keeps your last 3 looks per drawing tool, even after a restart. A CE key (on, dashed, off) keeps the line's color.
▸Drag the grip to park the bar; double-click it to make it follow again. Turn it on or off from the palette key's screen.
Omen DrawREDESIGN
▸Modes are screen keys now, with FORCE on its own, and the scope has its own SCOPE key.
▸Templates: save a whole setup and switch from the display, with UNDO and Ctrl+Z. Three starters to begin with: NY Intraday, Day draw and Session.
▸A new SETUP rail keeps Templates, Pools and Lines in one place (it replaces the POOLS card).
▸Scope settings open on a screen under the display, with LCD knobs.
▸Folded, the dash shows the next draw and its fuel (STAND DOWN when there's no draw). The status line tags the reach: green reachable, amber stretch, red too far.
Omen MTF CEREDESIGN
▸All 12 timeframes always in reach, in their own small display: two rows of six big keys with bigger up/down vote arrows.
▸LIVE, WICKWATCH and TARGET keys sit right on the display.
▸Templates, just like Position Sizer and Omen Layers.
▸Settings live on one rail (Templates, Flip Alert, On Chart), and the scope settings open on a screen with LCD knobs.
▸Flip alert gets SOUND and a watch/confirm line, and stays visible when the settings are folded.
Omen LayersREDESIGN
▸SHOW and TUNE are screen keys. TUNE is a rail of all 12 modules, with each value right on its key.
▸Templates for whole setups, hidden items included, with UNDO, Ctrl+Z and a star for your default. Ten ICT starters: Hangman (AM), NY Open Killzone, London Open Killzone, Market Open, Judas Swing, AM Silver Bullet, NY Lunch Raid, PM Silver Bullet, Final Hour and TGIF.
▸REMOVE one PD array at one timeframe in a single click, with UNDO.
▸A cleaner chart: big zones draw unfilled, other timeframes show just the CE line, lines break around their labels, and sizes scale for ES and NQ.
▸An FVG that inverts hands over to the IFVG layer, so inversions look the same everywhere. New default colors, too.
Omen SwitchREDESIGN
A new settings window.
▸Pages on a rail, like the rest of the Omen tools.
▸Click a theme or a color and the chart changes at once, with UNDO.
▸Saved themes keep your chart settings too.
▸Star a default theme for new charts (it replaces "Theme new charts automatically").
HOW TO UPDATE
 
1Open your Gumroad library and download the new zip for each tool you own.
2In NinjaTrader: Tools > Import > NinjaScript Add-On and pick the zip.
3While you're flat: reload every chart that has an Omen tool (right-click the chart > Reload NinjaScript, or press F5 on it), or restart NinjaTrader.
Open my Gumroad library →
Your update window in NinjaTrader will also tell you which of your tools have a new version.
