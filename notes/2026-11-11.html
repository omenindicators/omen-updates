WHAT'S NEW
 
Omen DashNEW 1.0.1.8 → 1.0.1.9
Your dashboard and journal, wherever you are, with nothing more to pay.
▸No subscription, no monthly fee. Remote access is part of your one-time Omen Dash purchase, and the price hasn't changed. Tailscale's personal plan is free.
▸Remote access. Open Omen Dash on your phone, tablet or laptop, from anywhere, through your own free Tailscale account.
▸One-click setup. Settings → Remote access checks Tailscale for you, turns it on in one click and shows your link as a QR code: scan it with your phone's camera and you're in. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.
▸Private by design. Your data never leaves your PC except to your own devices, over an encrypted connection. Only your own Tailscale account can open it; nothing goes through Omen.
▸Made for phones. The dashboard and journal fit phone screens: header actions in the ⋯ menu, a calendar that fits the width, full-screen windows, and Analytics with the results right under the picker.
▸Three new Soft themes (Theme → Soft): Soft Oi, Soft Violet and Soft Studio, with rounded glowing cards, pill-shaped meters and a gradient under your P&L curve.
▸Omen Dash always uses the same port (7878 unless you change it), so your bookmarks keep working, and it tells you if another program has it.
▸The user guide has a new step-by-step chapter: Remote access: Omen Dash on your phone.
Omen Position SizerNEW 1.0.2.0 → 1.0.2.1
Your flags, your colors.
▸Flag colors on SETUP → FLAGS: pick a set (Classic, Color-blind safe, Neon or Quiet) or save your own.
▸Seven colors, one key each: entry, stop, target, drag guide, snap glow, and the new long and short position lines. Click a key for 20 swatches or More colors… for any color.
▸The filled position line has its own color, so a long position never looks like your take profit again.
▸Readable on any color: flag text turns dark on light colors such as Neon's green.
▸Every Position Sizer chart shares your flag colors, and templates leave them alone. UNDO and Ctrl+Z put a change back.
Omen SwitchNEW 1.0.0.5 → 1.0.0.6
Fade the candle bodies, keep the wicks.
▸Body opacity on ⚙ → COLORS → CANDLES: a − / + stepper from 10% to 100%. No more typing color codes.
▸The outline and wick stay at full strength when the body is see-through. Switch puts the new Omen Candles bar style on the chart for you; charts with solid bodies stay on NinjaTrader's candles.
▸Outline and wick, right under the bodies: UP / DOWN COLOR follows each candle's color, OWN COLORS shows the outline and wick pickers right there.
▸The COLORS page is split into CHART and CANDLES, so every color is where you expect it. Picking a new up or down color keeps your body opacity, and themes save it.
